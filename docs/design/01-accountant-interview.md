# Interview guide: the accountant's routine (about 45 minutes)

Goal: write down how the money is checked **today**, step by step, so the app copies the
routine instead of inventing a new one. Ask the accountant to show their screen and the Excel
file while answering. Record answers (typed or voice) in `intake/notes/`.

Rule of thumb: ask for **the last real example**, not the general rule ("show me what you did
for yesterday's refund", not "how do you handle refunds").

## 1. The day (D+1)

1. What time do you start on the previous day's sales, and what do you open first?
2. Walk me through yesterday from first file to last. What do you receive from whom, and how
   (WhatsApp, email, paper)?
3. How long does it take on a normal day? On a bad day? What makes a day bad?
4. When is a day "closed" for you? Can it be reopened, and by whom?

## 2. Ticket screenshots and the RM line

5. What exactly is written in the RM line? (price only? price + client? partner name? payment
   method?) Show three real examples.
6. Does every agent write it the same way? What are the usual mistakes?
7. What do you do when a screenshot is missing or unreadable?
8. Are there sales with **no** ticket screenshot (visas, hotels, extras)? How do you learn about them?

## 3. The Amadeus daily report

9. Which lines do you look at, which do you ignore?
10. Refunds (RFND): how do you find the original ticket? Is the refund amount from the airline the
    same as what the client gets back? Who keeps the difference (penalty)?
11. Voids/cancellations (CANX): same day only? Does the client pay anything?
12. Extras / EMDs (bags, seats, change fees): how do they appear, and how are they priced?
13. Reissues/exchanges: how are the old and new tickets linked?

## 4. Your Excel sheet

14. Explain each column of your sheet. Which are typed by hand, which are formulas?
15. What totals do you send to the manager, and when?
16. What does "it doesn't match" usually mean, and how do you find the cause?
17. When you correct a line, do you keep a trace of the old value?

## 5. Clients, partners and invoices

18. Who are the partners? Do they pay per ticket, per week, per month?
19. When is an invoice made: at sale, end of day, end of month? Who signs it?
20. What must appear on an invoice (NIF, address, numbering, stamp, tax mentions)?
21. Due date: 15 days from **invoice date** or from **ticket date**?
22. Partial payments: allowed? How do you record them?
23. Reminders today: who calls, when, what is said? Who should get the "3 days left" alert?
24. A client who never paid: what happens?

## 6. Money in

25. List every way money comes in (cash, Bankily, Masrvi, Sedad, transfer, cheque, credit).
26. How do you match a mobile-money payment to a sale? (reference? name? amount only?)
27. Cash: who counts it, when, and where is the count written?
28. How do you pay the airlines (BSP, consolidator, prepaid deposit)? How often?

## 7. Revenue, cost, profit

29. For you, what is revenue: the full ticket price, or only the agency's margin?
30. Which costs count against profit (airline cost, partner commission, bank fees, staff
    commission)?
31. Do agents get a commission? On what?

## 8. Edits, mistakes, trust

32. Who is allowed to change a price after it was entered? Should the manager approve it?
33. What mistakes cost the agency money in the last months? (this tells us which checks matter most)

## 9. Wishes

34. If the app did **one** thing for you tomorrow, what would it be?
35. What must the app **never** do?

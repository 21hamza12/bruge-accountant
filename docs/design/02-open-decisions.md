# Open decisions, with a proposed default for each

Each line has a **proposed default** so design can continue. Tick it, or write the change.
Nothing here is built until approved.

| # | Question | Proposed default | Why | Needed by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where does the chat live? | **In the app** (installed on the phone like an app), not WhatsApp | WhatsApp Business API costs per message, needs Meta approval, and messages leave our control; the app already has logins and agency separation | Chat design |
| 2 | Pasted text as well as screenshots? | **Both**; pasted text preferred | Pasted text is read by plain code: free and exact. Screenshots stay as the fallback | Extraction design |
| 3 | Are screenshots kept? | **Decided: no.** The image is read in memory, the extracted fields are shown back to the staff member to confirm, then the image is discarded (never written to disk or the database). We keep only the confirmed fields, who confirmed them and when, and a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the image so the same screenshot is not entered twice | Owner's decision. Less personal data held. The proof of a price becomes the staff member's confirmation, not the picture | Storage |
| 4 | Who may edit a confirmed entry? | **Decided (owner, 26 Oct 2026, replacing "same day only")**: a sale is filed under the day its ticket was **issued**. Staff may enter, edit and void **their own sales** (own signs only) on **any day that is not closed** and not in the future; for a closed day they are told to ask the manager or the accountant, who reopen it as before. Manager and accountant: **any entry**, with a mandatory reason. Every edit kept in the history | Mistakes get fixed fast; nothing disappears | Ledger |
| 5 | Do edits on a closed day need approval? | **Yes, the manager approves** edits to a day the accountant has closed | Totals already reported must not move silently | Ledger |
| 6 | Invoice due date counted from? | **Invoice date + 15 days** | Standard practice; the interview confirms it | Invoices |
| 7 | Who gets the "due within 3 days" and "overdue" alerts? | **Manager and accountant**, in the app; overdue also in the daily report | Keeps reminders internal until we know how clients react | Invoices |
| 8 | Remind clients and partners directly? | **Not at first.** The app prepares the message; a person sends it | Avoid an automated message going wrong with a good partner | Invoices |
| 9 | Partial payments? | **Allowed**; the invoice stays open until fully paid | Usual with partners | Invoices |
| 10 | Partner billing | **Per ticket at sale, grouped on one invoice per partner per period** (period chosen per partner: weekly or monthly) | Fits both habits | Invoices |
| 11 | Payment methods | **Cash, Bankily, Masrvi, Sedad, bank transfer, cheque, on credit** (editable list per agency) | From the interview so far | Ledger |
| 12 | Voice messages | **Later** (Phase 4), not in the first chat version | Text and screenshots first; voice adds cost and errors | Chat |
| 13 | Revenue definition | **Revenue = P.B (Bruge price) of every sale. Gross profit = P.B − Amadeus cost (+ airline commission COMM).** For a partner sale this equals the partner fee; for a direct sale it is the amount the agent added. Margin = profit / revenue | P.B, AG, CLT, AP explained by the owner ([04-findings-first-files.md](04-findings-first-files.md) §5) | Reports |
| 14 | Does the AI ever set or suggest prices? | **Never sets a price.** Advice is limited to costs, late payers, trends; every suggestion is labelled as advice | Your rule: prices only come from staff | AI advice |
| 15 | AI model | **Haiku for routine reading, Sonnet for free questions and weekly advice**, decided after the golden-set test | Cost about $5–15/month per agency | Cost model |
| 16 | Airline code 495 | **Decided (owner, 27 Oct): Mauritania Airlines** (carrier code L6) | Shown by name on the dashboard, sales and reports (`app/airlines.py`) | Reports |
| 17 | Real staff names in the app | Use real names from the staff list (`intake/notes/`) | – | Accounts |
| 18 | Who approves a difference between the app report and the Amadeus report? | **The issuing staff member confirms** which value is right; **the accountant or manager validates** before a cost, ticket number or ticket type is corrected. A passenger-name spelling fix needs the staff member only | Cost and ticket changes move money and must match what the airline bills | Comparison |
| 19 | Partners: invoices with a 15-day deadline, or a running account? | **Running account per partner**, statement per half-month (as the BADI sheet does today); alert when a period's balance is unpaid 15 days after the period ends, critical 3 days before. Clients keep per-invoice due dates | The BADI file shows partners pay lump sums against a balance, not invoice by invoice ([04-findings-first-files.md](04-findings-first-files.md) §3) | Invoices |
| 20 | The existing billing software is custom and cannot be linked: what happens to invoices? | **Decided (owner, 21 Oct): the app takes over** client invoices, unpaid-invoice lists and client statements (same documents and agency details as today), and partner statements. While both run, staff stop typing into the old software for each module the app takes over, so nothing is typed twice | The old software cannot export or connect; running both in parallel for long means double entry and two different balances | Invoices |
| 21 | One RM price for several tickets: how is P.B shown per line in the TJQ APRES? | **Decided (owner, 22 Oct): full price on the first ticket, 0 on the others**, lines marked as one sale ("Sale #12, 2 tickets") | Totals stay right, no invented per-ticket prices, matches the single RM price; only the export changes if the accountant prefers another layout | B10 export |
| 22 | Partner sale with several tickets: fee per ticket or per sale? | **Decided (owner, 22 Oct): per ticket**: P.B = Σ cost + fee × number of tickets | Matches the BADI file, where every ticket line has its own Frais BRG | B4 rules |
| 23 | Go live with or without the chat and screenshots? | **Decided (owner, 22 Oct): with them, from day one.** Order: B → D (screenshots) → E (chat for agents, accountant, manager) → C (go live + parallel run). Pasting PNR text is a second way in | Agents learn one way of working from the start; costs about a week before go-live | Build order |
| 24 | Who may see the profit? | **Decided (owner, 28 Oct): the manager only.** The accountant and the agents never see the profit or margin: no « Profit » column, row value, total or summary, no profit in chat answers, and the TJQ APRES margin row (« P.B − TOTAL DOC ») only in the manager's download. P.B and the Amadeus cost stay visible to whoever sees them today (the owner accepts that the accountant could subtract them), and the accountant still sees partner fees for the partner statements. Stored TJQ APRES versions keep the row; it is removed when the accountant downloads. One check: `permissions.can_see_profit` | The profit is the owner's business; the accountant needs P.B, cost and fees for her own work, not the profit itself | All pages, chat, B10 export |
| 25 | A client without a name; tickets issued before today in a paste or a screenshot | **Decided (owner, 29 Oct): « CLIENT », and old tickets are not read.** In the owner's words: "if the client does not have a name put CLIENT and if the date is date older than the today date dont read the ticket with the old date from the pasted or from the screenshot". (1) A direct sale whose CLT is empty (left blank on the form, or no client in the paste or screenshot) is stored as « CLIENT » by the server (`sales_rules.DEFAULT_CLIENT`); paste and screenshot proposals read as a direct sale (BRUGE) are prefilled with it (an unknown agency is not, since it may be answered "partner"). A typed name is kept; a partner sale still needs no client name. (2) Reading a paste or a screenshot ignores every ticket issued before today (the application clock's UTC date, Nouakchott time), for every role; one notice names them, « N billet(s) émis avant aujourd'hui ignoré(s) : numéros », and when every ticket is old that notice is the whole answer. A ticket whose issue date cannot be read is still proposed and goes to today, with its notice. A proposal that lost some of its tickets this way but still shows the RM price says « P.B lu sur la réservation entière : vérifiez qu'il ne compte pas les billets ignorés ». A list of proposals carried for « déjà saisie » that was made on an earlier day is refused (paste again). The hand-typed form is unchanged, so a late ticket can still be entered by hand (a manager or the accountant choose its day). This replaces the « Date d'émission ancienne » notice (more than 31 days back). The signed issue date (`app/issue_sig.py`) still binds today's tickets | Every direct sale has a client to show; a ticket from an earlier day is usually an old one shown again in the booking (an exchanged original), not a sale of today | Sale entry, paste and screenshot reading |
| 26 | May the agents see the totals of cost and of P.B? | **Decided (owner, 30 Oct): no.** In the owner's words: "i dont want the agent to see cout total or PB total if they can see it they can calculate the profit". Agents (role staff) never see a total of cost (« Coût total ») or of P.B (« P.B total ») over several sales: not in « Mes ventes », not in the « Journées » list (no Coût and P.B columns), not on the day page (day totals and per-sign Coût and P.B), not on their home page (the old « Coût de mes billets » tile), not in a chat answer; the totals are not even computed for them. They keep the per-sale Coût and P.B columns, the cost and P.B of a single sale they enter or open (labelled « Coût de la vente » for everybody), the per-ticket costs, and the counts of sales and tickets. The accountant keeps every total (not the profit, decision 24); the manager sees everything. The close page and the TJQ APRES were already manager and accountant only. One check: `permissions.can_see_totals` | With a P.B total and a cost total side by side, the profit is one subtraction away | Sales and day pages, home, chat |
